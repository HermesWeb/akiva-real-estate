Structuring Joint Ventures in Lagos

Navigating land acquisition and development partnerships requires precision. For landowners with premium assets and developers with capital, the Joint Venture (JV) remains the most powerful tool for scaling in the Lagos market.

However, a successful JV is entirely dependent on the legal and structural foundation built before ground is ever broken. The primary points of failure in Lagos JVs often stem from misaligned valuation metrics or unclear sharing formulas on premium sharing ratios.

Mitigating Risk in Land Partnerships

At Akivarealty.co, we approach Joint Ventures by first verifying the integrity of the land title (C of O, Governor's Consent) and running comprehensive feasibility studies on the proposed residential build. We ensure the Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) created for the project protects both the landowner's equity and the developer's ROI timeline.
